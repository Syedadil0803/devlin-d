Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device - computer, tablet or smartphone - when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

Cookies are not harmful and cannot access any other information on your device. They simply help websites remember your preferences and understand how visitors interact with the site.

Some cookies are essential for the website to function properly and cannot be switched off. Others are optional and will only be activated with your explicit consent.

How We Use Cookies

We use a small number of cookies on our website. We do not use cookies for advertising or to track your behaviour across other websites. The cookies we use fall into the following categories:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are essential for our website to function correctly. Without these cookies certain features of the website would not work and the site may not perform as intended.

These cookies do not collect any personally identifiable information and cannot be switched off as they are integral to how the website operates. No consent is required for these cookies under UK GDPR and PECR.

Examples of what strictly necessary cookies do on our website include:

  • Remembering your cookie consent preferences
  • Enabling secure browsing sessions
  • Ensuring the website loads and navigates correctly

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies enable key features on our website including our interactive showroom map, virtual tour, and any social sharing or feedback tools. These cookies are provided by trusted third party services and are only activated with your explicit consent. Declining these cookies will not affect your ability to browse our website.

We currently use the following third-party tools that may set functional cookies:

Google Maps
We embed Google Maps on our website to help you locate and navigate to our Devlin Wholesale showroom locations. If you choose to interact with the map, Google may set cookies to enable this functionality. These cookies are controlled by Google and we have no access to or control over them. For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Matterport
We embed a Matterport virtual tour on our website to allow you to explore our showroom remotely before visiting us in person. If you choose to view the virtual tour, Matterport may set cookies to enable this experience. These cookies are controlled by Matterport and we have no access to or control over them. For more information, please refer to Matterport's Privacy Policy at matterport.com/privacy-policy.

You can choose to accept or decline functional cookies at any time through our cookie consent settings. Declining these cookies will prevent the interactive map and virtual tour from loading, but will not affect your ability to use the rest of our website.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

We currently do not use performance or analytics cookies on this website. Should this change in the future we will update this policy and ask for your explicit consent before any performance or analytics cookies are set.

Third Party Cookies

Some pages on our website embed content or tools provided by third parties such as Google and Matterport. These third parties may set their own cookies independently of our cookie consent system. We recommend reviewing their individual privacy policies for full details of how they handle your data.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Through Our Website

You can update or withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clicking the Cookie Settings link in the footer of our website. Changes will take effect immediately and do not require you to adjust your browser settings.

Through Your Browser

You can also control or delete cookies directly through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies at any time. Please refer to your browser's help or settings menu for guidance on how to manage cookies on your device. Please note that disabling certain cookies through your browser may affect how our website functions, including the ability to view our interactive map and virtual showroom tour.

Third Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are set by third party services including Google Maps and Matterport. These are managed independently by those providers. To opt out of their cookies directly please refer to their individual privacy policies linked in the Third-Party Cookies section above.

Updates to This Policy

Devlin Wholesale may update this cookie policy from time to time to reflect changes in the tools we use or in applicable legislation.

Where changes are significant, we will notify you by displaying a prominent notice on our website or by re-presenting the cookie consent popup so you can review and update your preferences.

We encourage you to check this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
